About the Olist Dataset
The Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset from Kaggle contains 100,000+ real orders placed across Brazil between 2016–2018. The schema consists of 9 normalized relational tables including orders, order items, customers, sellers, products, payments, reviews, geolocation, and categories — making it ideal for advanced SQL analytics covering supply chain, CRM, and business intelligence.

SP vs AL: The Delivery Divide
São Paulo (SP) delivers in 8.8 days avg with only 5.88% late rate — versus Alagoas (AL) at 24.5 days avg and 23.94% late rate. This 2.78× delivery time gap directly explains the 1.72-star rating difference and suggests prioritizing last-mile infrastructure investment in northeastern Brazil (AL, MA, PI, CE, SE).
